The team behind Voice First Notion

Developed by the R&D team at CfHE.

Voice First Notion brings together a tactile handheld controller and a companion audiobook app, developed by the research and development team at the Center for Healthcare Entrepreneurship (CfHE), IIT Hyderabad.

Current stage · user testing

Built. Now being tested with users.

The tactile remote and companion mobile application have reached the user-testing stage. This phase focuses on learning from real use, evaluating accessibility and refining the overall experience. The product is not yet commercially available.

What has been developed

  • Physical remote designs
  • Mobile app with home, library, player and bookmark screens
  • Integrated listening, bookmarking and note-taking experience for user evaluation

What user testing is evaluating

  • Reliable remote-to-app operation and button mapping
  • Speech capture, transcription behavior and note review
  • Ease of use and accessibility for blind and low-vision listeners
  • Reliability, device compatibility, privacy and readiness for wider release

Frequently asked questions

What does Voice First Notion aim to do?

What is Voice First Notion?

An assistive audiobook listening and voice-note system developed by CfHE, combining a tactile remote with a mobile application. It is currently undergoing user testing.

Can the remote control the app?

The remote is designed to operate the companion app. The combined interaction is being evaluated during user testing; performance across devices and use cases is not yet confirmed.

Can I speak a note instead of typing?

Voice note-taking is part of the product experience being evaluated. The video illustrates spoken words appearing as text; that specific transcription capability is not verified by the supplied screenshots.

Can I revisit the part of the audiobook I marked?

The actual app screenshots show a bookmarks list and player controls. User testing is evaluating the complete note-review experience through the remote.

Can I buy it now?

Not yet. Voice First Notion is at the user-testing stage. A release date, price and preorder offering have not been announced.

Can I watch a demonstration of the device?

Yes. Play the embedded video on this page to see the product demonstration and explanation. If playback requires access, the CfHE team will need to update the video’s Google Drive sharing settings.
